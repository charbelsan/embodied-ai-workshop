# Access to your machine (participant sheet)

1. Open your team's address in **Chrome, Edge or Firefox** (nothing to install): `https://<address>:8443` (given with your `vinci-tNN` user name and your password).
2. **Certificate warning** (normal: temporary machine, self-signed certificate). Click:
   - Chrome: "Your connection is not private" → **Advanced** → **Proceed to … (unsafe)**;
   - Edge: "Your connection isn't private" → **Advanced** → **Continue to … (unsafe)**;
   - Firefox: "Warning: Potential Security Risk Ahead" → **Advanced…** → **Accept the Risk and Continue**.
3. Your team's user name and password → the desktop appears with **Isaac Sim already open**. Its window covers the desktop icons: minimize it (**_** button, top right) or move it, then double-click **START WORKSHOP**.
4. The notebook and the terminal are on the desktop. If something breaks: ask an organizer for a **reset** (≈ 1 min, your machine is not restarted).
5. Do not share your password with another team: each team has its own machine.
